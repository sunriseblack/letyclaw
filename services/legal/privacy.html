Privacy Policy — Letyclaw

Last updated: 2026-08-10

Letyclaw is a self-hosted personal automation assistant. Each deployment is controlled by its operator and is not offered to third parties by the Letyclaw project.

Who uses Letyclaw

Only the operator and Google accounts explicitly connected by that operator. The operator controls who can interact with their deployment.

What data Letyclaw accesses via Google

Letyclaw connects to the operator's own Gmail accounts via the Gmail API using the scopes gmail.send, gmail.compose, and gmail.modify to:

  • Compose drafts on behalf of the operator
  • Send messages from the operator's own accounts
  • Manage (read / update / delete) drafts the application itself created

How data is stored

OAuth credentials are stored locally on infrastructure controlled by the operator and protected with restrictive filesystem permissions. Host or disk encryption is the operator's responsibility. Telegram messages, agent responses, and scrubbed conversation-recall records may be retained locally according to the deployment's configured session and log retention.

Processors and connected services

A deployment sends data only to services the operator configures, such as Telegram, Anthropic/Claude, Google, OpenAI, Vapi, or other MCP integrations. Those services process data under their own terms. The Letyclaw project does not operate a central service or receive a copy of self-hosted deployment data.

What Letyclaw does NOT do

  • Letyclaw does not operate a central database of Google user data.
  • Letyclaw does not use Google user data for advertising.
  • Letyclaw does not sell Google user data.
  • Access to a self-hosted deployment is controlled by its operator.

Retention & deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time from the operator's Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation prevents future Google API access. The operator must separately remove local token, session, log, recall, backup, and generated-artifact files according to their retention requirements.

Contact

Questions about a deployment should be directed to that deployment's operator.
